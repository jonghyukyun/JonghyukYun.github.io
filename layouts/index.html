Jonghyuk Yun

Ph.D. student at KAIST

I am a Ph.D. student at the Sensing Intelligence and Cyber-Physical Security (CyPhy) Lab in the School of Computing, KAIST, advised by Prof. Jun Han.

Interest: Mobile and Sensing Systems for Public Safety
My research interest lies in the novel use of sensors within mobile environments to tackle challenges pertinent to public safety. In particular, I am keen on developing novel solutions for security and safety issues related to public safety by augmenting physics models and integrating diverse sensing modalities.

I received my B.S. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Konkuk University (2022) and my M.S. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Yonsei University (2024).
